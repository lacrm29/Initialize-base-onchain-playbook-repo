
# Daily Activity

## Consistency

Maintaining regular activity across different days creates a more balanced interaction history.
Short but repeated sessions appear more sustainable than occasional high-volume usage.
Daily engagement also improves familiarity with the ecosystem over time.

## Transaction Flow

Small transactions help maintain ongoing interaction without unnecessary spending.
Using different tokens and protocols increases diversity within the activity profile.
Regular transaction flow also reflects continued ecosystem participation.

## Long-Term Approach

Long-term consistency is more effective than temporary bursts of activity.
Gradual interaction growth creates a more natural onchain pattern across time.
A steady approach also allows better tracking of changes within the Base ecosystem.
